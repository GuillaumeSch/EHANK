\appendix
\section*{Appendix}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Appendix}

\noindent The appendices collect supporting material. Appendix~\ref{app:booking}
derives the balance-of-payments booking of the adoption flows and shows how the
booking convention signs the adoption channel. Appendix~\ref{app:nonlin} reports
the accuracy of the linearised solution and the nonlinear welfare check. Appendix~\ref{sec:monetary}
develops the monetary analysis: the adoption margin leaves monetary transmission
unchanged, while the monetary stance moves the adoption wave only weakly relative to
the fiscal instruments. Appendix~\ref{app:taste} treats the logit taste scale as both
a numerical smoothing device and a structural adoption elasticity, and reports its
identification.
