  psi_g and taste_shock (σ_ε) enter the logit only through psi_g/σ and 1/σ; σ_ε is
  not separately pinned. Structural adoption-price-elasticity floor ≈ −4 on the

## 6. Roadmap to submission (my proposed staging)

`\NEEDSRERUN`, delete the wrappers; apply audit patches A2/A4; circulate the ~7×
magnitude changes to Boris and Francesca and lock the headline tables.

**Phase 1 — credibility core (the referee's first objections).**
1. **Identification / robustness of (psi_g, σ_ε)**: a sensitivity table showing the
   headline numbers (Δ₂₄, the cap-vs-transfer CEV gap, the persistence result) across
   an empirically defensible range of psi_g and σ_ε. Report the model-implied
   adoption elasticity and confront it with Muehlegger–Rapson / Beresteanu–Li.
2. **Empirical anchoring / motivation**: one figure of actual EU heat-pump / EV
   adoption around 2022–23 (did the crisis accelerate the transition?) to ground
   D_GREEN=0.05, ϱ=0.8, δ_g and to motivate the mechanism from data.
3. **Validation against the three references**: make explicit that the frozen model,
   under the elastic vs inelastic closure, reproduces the Langot vs Bayer signs — the
   paper "nests by omission," and showing the recovery is the cleanest defence.

**Phase 2 — referee-proofing (the deeper objections).**
4. **Non-homothetic energy robustness**: re-run §5.5 with a necessity energy nest;
   report whether the regressive result survives. This is the paper's most exposed claim.
5. **Social cost of carbon on the ex-ante result**: the "ex-ante greening is
   counterproductive" claim is about *private* insurance value only; permanent
   greening lowers emissions the private CEV ignores. Add a back-of-envelope: at what
   SCC does ex-ante greening break even against ex-post insurance? Honest and strong.
6. **Nonlinear headline**: solve the main price-shock experiment nonlinearly at a
   defensible size (or bound the linear error), given NL/L up to 1.67.

**Phase 3 — packaging.**
7. **Target-journal decision** (shapes length/framing): field outlets fit best —
   JEEM, EER, or a monetary/JMCB-type. Decide before polishing the intro.
8. Tighten the Model section (push derivations to an appendix); polish intro/abstract;
   final consistency pass on numbers and cross-refs.

Ordering priority if time is short: Phase 0 → 1.1 (identification) → 2.4
(non-homothetic) → 2.5 (SCC). Those four are what make or break credibility.
